About this system

Who, how, what changed when.

The When design system is a living thing. People own it, others contribute, and every release is documented. This page is the door for anyone, internal or external, who wants to know how the system works and where to plug in.

01, Governance

Owners.

Each surface of the system has a primary owner. The owner is the person to ask, the person who reviews changes, and the person who decides when a change ships. Roles below are placeholders, drop actual contacts into _inbox/exec-bios/owners.md and the next pass populates this table.

Brand

Owner pending

Verbal identity, voice, messaging architecture, naming, boilerplate. Approves any change to how the company sounds in public.

Visual identity

Owner pending

Logo, color, type, iconography, imagery, motion principles. Owns the brand-mark download pack and any rendering of the wordmark.

Design system

Owner pending

Tokens, components, patterns, modules. Reviews every new primitive before it lands in showcase.css.

Accessibility

Owner pending

WCAG audit, keyboard nav, screen-reader patterns, reduced-motion compliance. Audits every release.

Content + voice

Owner pending

Member voice, sales voice, smell-test reviews. Final word on copy across surfaces.

Engineering integration

Owner pending

CSS token export, code snippets, Figma library sync, future Storybook / npm package work.

02, Governance

Contribute.

Anyone can propose a change. Small things go straight to the owner. New components, new tokens, or anything that touches multiple surfaces goes through a short RFC.

Small change, single owner

A copy tweak, a contrast fix, a new icon mapping, a missing state. Slack the owner, share a screenshot, ship within the week.

New primitive or breaking change, RFC

Anything that affects more than one surface, or changes the contract of an existing primitive, goes through an RFC. Use the template below.

# RFC, [name of the change]

**Owner:** [your name]
**Reviewers:** [primary owner + 1 cross-functional]
**Status:** Draft / In review / Approved / Shipped / Withdrawn
**Target release:** v0.X

## Why
[The problem. Be specific. Cite the surfaces affected.]

## What
[The change. New primitive, modified primitive, removed primitive.]

## Impact
[What breaks. What migrates. What documentation needs updating.]

## Open questions
[Anything you don't have an answer to yet.]

## Alternatives considered
[Why this approach over the others.]

Where to file

For now: drop RFCs into _inbox/messaging/rfcs/[rfc-name].md. Once the system is canonical, RFCs move to a dedicated repo with versioning + review workflow.

Feedback channel

Found a mistake? Have an idea? The fastest path is whoever owns the relevant surface (table above). Cross-cutting feedback or anything you're not sure where to land goes to the brand owner.

Changelog.

Material changes to tokens, components, modules, voice, or any other surface. Newest first. Breaking changes called out explicitly.

v0.5 · May 2026

Brand foundation lands. Full brand.html page with overview, audiences, story, naming, messaging architecture, and boilerplate. Section-tabs strip becomes content-sized pills with elastic spring transition (pill ↔ circle on container width). Button motion fixed, press uses emphasized curve (no overshoot), shape-morph corner-full to corner-md, scale 0.96. Voice · Sales page drafted as a parallel to Voice · Member. Comprehensive motion section added.

v0.3 · May 2026

Brand palette pulled from styleguide reference: Radiance #CC4400, Shine #FFBE00, Cloud #496767, Linen surface family. Fonts swapped from Roboto to Roboto Flex (later corrected to Google Sans Text + Merriweather everywhere serif).

v0.2 · May 2026

Voice · Member page shipped: 6 sections (Voice, Tone, Words, In practice, Mechanics, Speakers). MD3 Expressive audit completed, 92 deltas catalogued.

v0.1 · April 2026

Foundation: shell, rail, subnav, section-tabs, right-pane. Token architecture (md.ref → md.sys → md.comp). Initial component set: buttons, cards, chips, inputs, dialogs, snackbar.

Versioning

Semantic-ish: 0.X while the system is pre-1.0 and breaking changes can happen without a major bump. Each release notes breaking changes explicitly. 1.0 lands when the brand owner signs off + an external partner has built against the system.
